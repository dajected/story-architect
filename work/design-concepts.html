The Glass Harbour

After the storm

Mara stopped beneath the clock tower. Salt had dried along the sleeves of her coat, leaving pale lines where the water had reached. Across the harbour, a light turned in the abandoned lighthouse.

It had been dark for seventeen years.

She set her bag on the wet stone and watched it sweep across the water again. Once, twice. The same patient rhythm she remembered from childhood, when her father was still alive and every boat in the harbour carried a name she knew.

Behind her, someone unlocked the door of the old customs house. She did not turn. Not yet.
