The harbour had forgotten how to be quiet. Even now, with the storm moving east, the boats pulled at their moorings and the loose shutters spoke to one another across the street.

Mara stopped beneath the clock tower. Salt had dried along the sleeves of her coat. Her left hand throbbed inside its makeshift bandage.

Across the water, a light turned in the abandoned lighthouse.

“That,” Elias said, “wasn’t there yesterday.”
